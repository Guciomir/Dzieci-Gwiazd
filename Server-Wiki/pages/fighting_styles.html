Style walki

Zmiany w stylach walki są obowiązkowe na serwerze. Style zmienione na serwerze lub niedostępne standardowo w Shard Tabletop mają oznaczenie (G) w paczce serwerowej.

Zmiany w oficjalnych stylach

Two-Weapon Fighting

When you engage in two-weapon fighting, add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

Server rule. When you take the Attack action on your turn and qualify for the off-hand melee weapon attack, you can make it without spending a bonus action.

Dozwolone style homebrew

Close Quarters Shooter

A hostile creature within 5 feet of you does not impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls. Against targets within 30 feet, your ranged weapon attacks ignore half cover and treat three-quarters cover as half cover.

Klasy Ranger, Fighter, Blood Hunter.
